# BNI 5-Min Presentation Structure

15-slide canonical structure used in BNI weekly presentations. Adapted from real BNI Thailand decks (e.g. PONIX EV Chargers — see `~ Human Briefing/` in the `85 BNI 5-Min Presentation` project).

When the audience is **BNI**, use this slide order verbatim. The order is purposeful: each slide does a job that the next slide builds on.

## The 15 slides

| # | Slide | Template | Job to be done |
|---|-------|----------|----------------|
| 1 | **Cover** | T01 cover | Brand + ฉายา + first impression |
| 2 | **About** | T03 stat-grid (or T05 two-column) | Establish credibility (years, team, location) |
| 3 | **Why Choose Us** | T03 stat-grid | Hard proof points (awards, scale) |
| 4 | **Services Overview** | T04 feature-grid | What you sell, in 3 buckets |
| 5 | **LCD** | T02 statement | The one line they must remember |
| 6 | **Service Detail #1** | T05 two-column | Deep dive on service 1 + examples |
| 7 | **Service Detail #2** | T05 two-column | Deep dive on service 2 + examples |
| 8 | **Our Strengths** | T04 feature-grid | Differentiation (not just what — *why us*) |
| 9 | **Customers** | T06 logo-grid | Social proof |
| 10 | **Case Study** | T07 case-study | One hero project in detail |
| 11 | **Looking For** | T08 callout-list | The personas you want referrals to |
| 12 | **Trigger Words** | T09 trigger-list | "When you hear X, think of me" |
| 13 | **ถาม / เชีย / นัด** | T10 script-box | Literal script for members to use |
| 14 | **Misconceptions** | T11 comparison | Address objections that block referrals |
| 15 | **Closing** | T12 closing | Big name + contact + farewell |

## The "must not skip" slides

If the timer is short and you have to cut, these 6 are the ones that **make the deck a BNI deck** (not just a generic pitch):

1. **Cover** (#1) — establishes ฉายา BNI
2. **LCD** (#5) — the memorable one-liner
3. **Looking For** (#11) — without this, members don't know who to refer
4. **Trigger Words** (#12) — what to listen for
5. **ถาม/เชีย/นัด** (#13) — exact words to say
6. **Closing** (#15) — how to hand off

Cut from #2-4, #6-10, #14 first if you must.

## Tone notes for BNI

- **Use the presenter's ฉายา prominently.** Repeat it on Cover, LCD, Triggers, and Closing. The whole point is members remember the ฉายา.
- **"Looking For" must be specific.** "Anyone who needs tech" → bad. "SME owners who want a custom system but have no dev team" → good. Specific personas are referrable; vague ones aren't.
- **Trigger words are literal phrases.** "ทำ MVP", "ไม่มี dev team", "อยากทำเว็บ" — the exact words a customer would say. Members listen for these in the wild.
- **The script (T10) is for the BNI member to use, not the presenter.** Write it in the BNI member's voice: "พอดีรู้จัก [ฉายา] ที่ [บริษัท]…"
- **Misconceptions (T11) addresses why people *don't* refer to you.** Common: "I don't know if my contact needs it." Counter with crisp examples.

## Workflow when audience = BNI

1. Confirm presenter's **ฉายา** — this is the single most important content piece for a BNI deck. Without it, the deck doesn't function.
2. Confirm **3 service buckets** for the Services Overview slide.
3. Confirm **target personas** for "Looking For" — at least 2, ideally 3-4.
4. Confirm **3 trigger phrases** — literal words.
5. Use the script (T10) template; adapt the ถาม/เชีย/นัด lines to the presenter's industry.
